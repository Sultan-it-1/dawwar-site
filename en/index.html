What your PC needs

  • Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit
  • 4 GB of memory, 8 GB recommended
  • About 650 MB of disk space

Notes

  • Some Word, Excel and PowerPoint conversions are more faithful when Microsoft Office is installed; without it Dawwar uses its built-in engines.
  • Saving pictures as HEIC needs the “HEVC Video Extensions” from the Microsoft Store.
  • Arabic text recognition needs the Arabic language pack in Windows.
